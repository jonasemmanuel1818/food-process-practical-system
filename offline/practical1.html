1. Laboratory Orientation

Laboratory orientation introduces students to the food processing laboratory environment, the equipment used in practical work, health and safety requirements, and the rules that should be followed during laboratory activities.

Students should understand the laboratory environment before beginning practical experiments. Proper preparation and observation of safety procedures help to protect students, laboratory personnel, equipment and experimental materials.

2. Objectives

  1. To become familiar with the food processing laboratory environment.
  2. To identify health and safety equipment available in the laboratory.
  3. To understand laboratory rules and safety procedures.
  4. To understand the importance of following safety procedures during practical activities.

3. Health and Safety Equipment

Appropriate safety equipment should be available and used when carrying out laboratory activities. Students should know the purpose of the available safety equipment before starting practical work.

Laboratory Coat Used to protect clothing and the body from accidental spills and contamination.
Protective Gloves Used where appropriate to protect the hands during laboratory activities.
Safety Goggles Used to protect the eyes from splashes, particles and other laboratory hazards.
Laboratory Footwear Appropriate footwear helps protect the feet from spills, broken materials and other laboratory hazards.
First Aid Equipment First aid equipment should be accessible for responding to minor injuries and emergencies.

4. Laboratory Rules

  1. Follow all instructions given by the lecturer, laboratory instructor or responsible laboratory personnel.
  2. Wear appropriate protective clothing and equipment when required.
  3. Keep the laboratory working area clean and organized.
  4. Do not eat, drink or engage in activities that may compromise laboratory safety.
  5. Handle laboratory equipment carefully and according to the provided instructions.
  6. Report accidents, injuries, spills or damaged equipment immediately.
  7. Dispose of laboratory waste using the appropriate procedures.
  8. Wash hands after completing laboratory work.
  9. Do not operate equipment without proper instruction or authorization.
  10. Leave the laboratory clean and properly organized after completing practical work.

5. Safety Procedures

Before Starting Practical Work

  • Read and understand the practical instructions.
  • Identify possible hazards associated with the activity.
  • Check the condition of laboratory equipment before use.
  • Wear the required protective equipment.

During Practical Work

  • Follow the correct experimental procedure.
  • Handle equipment and materials carefully.
  • Keep the working area organized.
  • Report any unsafe condition immediately.

After Practical Work

  • Switch off equipment where applicable.
  • Clean the working area.
  • Return equipment to its appropriate location.
  • Dispose of waste appropriately.
  • Wash hands before leaving the laboratory.

6. Key Points to Remember

  • Safety is an essential part of laboratory work.
  • Laboratory equipment should only be used correctly and carefully.
  • Personal protective equipment should be used when required.
  • Accidents and unsafe conditions should be reported immediately.
  • The laboratory should be left clean and organized after practical work.
